# PD2AF working pages

Pages in progress, not linked from the official site and not indexed by search engines. The official pages stay as
they are until a change is decided.

- [Conversion rules, draft](/pd2af/rules/): our rules PD to Activity Flow to Boolean, each with its options,
  compared with CaSQ, the PD2AF 1.0 specification and pd2af 0.1.0; questions on the specification.
- [Specification, working copy](/pd2af/specification/): the PD2AF 1.0 specification, to be changed as rules are
  decided.

Empty pages, kept so the structure is not forgotten:

- [Systems compared](/pd2af/comparison/): The systems side by side: CaSQ, the PD2AF 1.0 specification, pd2af 0.1.0 (Rougny) and ours, what each reads and writes, and where each stops (PD to AF, or PD to Boolean).
- [What a good conversion is](/pd2af/criteria/): The criteria a conversion is judged by (no invented source, regulated removal expressed, the logic drawn is the logic read, ...), each with the systems that pass or fail it.
- [Cases](/pd2af/cases/): One row per PD pattern: what every system makes of it, the evaluation and the rule it decides. Waits on the decision whether results from unpublished modules may be shown here.
- [Examples](/pd2af/examples/): Whole modules converted with our rules: the PD map, the Activity Flow, the Boolean rules and the scenarios, beside the outputs of the other systems. The counterpart of the official Examples page.
- [Converter](/pd2af/converter/): The engine once it exists: a PD file in, Activity Flow and Boolean rules out, with the rule choices switchable. The counterpart of the old web service.
- [Development](/pd2af/development/): The state of the work, what is decided and what is open, and how the rules and the engine are developed. The counterpart of the official Development page.
- [About](/pd2af/about/): Who works on this and how it relates to the PD2AF 1.0 project and to pd2af 0.1.0. The counterpart of the official About page.
